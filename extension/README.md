# kth-grades Ladok Connector (browser extension)

Adds a floating "Send to kth-grades" button on `student.ladok.se`. Click it
after logging in and viewing your results, and your grades land in the
kth-grades app automatically — no password, cookie, or session token ever
touches this extension or the app.

## Install (Chrome/Edge, unpacked)

1. Go to `chrome://extensions`
2. Enable **Developer mode** (top right)
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select this `extension/` folder
4. Log in to Ladok at https://www.student.ladok.se, go to *Studieresultat*
5. Click the blue "📥 Send to kth-grades" button in the bottom-right corner

The app tab (kth-grades.github.io or your local dev server) opens or focuses
automatically and imports the courses.
